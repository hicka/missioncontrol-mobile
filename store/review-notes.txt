Mission Control Mobile is the companion to Mission Control, a free open-source agent that runs on the user's own Mac (https://github.com/hicka/missioncontrol). The phone connects to that Mac directly, by the address and access token shown in the Mac app. There is no account and no server of ours in between.

To review without a Mac, tap "Look around with sample data" on the first screen. Every screen then shows a realistic sample: the agent's home, its conversation, an Inbox with a held command and a question, tasks with their finish lines, and settings. Actions that would change something on a Mac say that nothing ran.

NSAllowsArbitraryLoads: the app talks to a server on the user's own Mac at a local network or Tailscale address, which cannot carry a public TLS certificate.
